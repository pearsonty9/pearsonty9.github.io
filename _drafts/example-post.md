This is a draft. Files in `_drafts/` are never published. To publish a post,
move it to `_posts/` and rename it `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md`, e.g.
`_posts/2026-10-21-making-dilemma.md`.

Preview drafts locally with `bundle exec jekyll serve --drafts`.
